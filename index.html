Does a packed stadium win baseball games?

Home teams win more. Do the fans explain it?

Conventional wisdom says KBO clubs play better at home, and that a packed stadium is what lifts them. We tested both halves of that belief with sixteen seasons of home/away records, batting and pitching splits, and new stadium, travel, city and climate features.

Method & limitationsWhat the data can and cannot say

Revelation

    Limitations

    • Team-season aggregates (n = 152) hide game-level detail such as starting pitcher, rest days and weather on the day.
    • Team-level features (shared stadium, dome, city climate) vary across only 10 clubs, so they are confounded with club identity. Kiwoom is the only dome team.
    • Stadium capacities are nominal era values; historical re-seating is not modelled, so occupancy is approximate.
    • Season travel distances were transcribed from VISUAL BASEBALL screenshots; their counting basis is not documented.
    • Climate uses 1991–2020 city normals, not the weather of each season.
    • 2020 has only 10 team-seasons, so its interval is wide. Stands were closed until late July 2020 and then only intermittently opened at 10–30% (with fresh closures from mid-August), so “near-empty” rather than empty.
    • Repeated clubs: the 152 team-seasons come from only 10 clubs, so they are not fully independent. Treating each club as a cluster, the home edge still holds (+4.5 pts, 95% CI +3.0 to +6.1, t = 6.7, df = 9, p < 0.001).
    • Multiple testing: we ran 22 tests. After Holm correction the home edge and the 2020 result still hold, but the shared-stadium result (p = 0.016) does not (Holm p ≈ 0.20); treat it as exploratory.
    • Data are reused for education with credit to their sources (KBO via Yagoonara and MyKBO Stats, kbo-crowd, KMA, Wikipedia); copyright stays with those sources.

    Data provenance

    • Team-season W-L-T, 2010–2025: Yagoonara team pages (KBO records). Cross-checked for 2014–2025 against MyKBO Stats and Wikipedia season pages: 119 of 119 team-seasons match.
    • Home/away batting and pitching splits: Yagoonara team pages (see the Metric_Source and Split_Source_URL columns).
    • Attendance: KBO historical attendance; 2023–2025 cross-checked against Wikipedia and KBO year-end figures.
    • 3,494 games, 2021–2025: the open kbo-crowd project (github.com/KENNYSOFT/kbo-crowd, commit 1d28725), built from KBO daily crowd records and the KBO schedule. Every row has a game ID, date, both teams and the final score, so any game can be checked against KBO box scores.
    • Weather is measured, not estimated: KMA ASOS hourly observations at the city station mapped to each stadium, taken at the first-pitch hour; rain = sum of the 3 hourly readings from first pitch. Missing readings are left blank.
    • Estimated fields: occupancy (crowd ÷ nominal capacity) and the COVID flag (all 2021 games; 2022 opened at 100% capacity).
    • Rebuild: analysis/00_build_games.py recreates the game file from kbo-crowd; 05_game_level.py reproduces every game-level number.

    Future research

    • More seasons of LG–Doosan head-to-heads: the Jamsil derby test (5th inning) points the right way but has only 77 games, so it is not yet significant.
    • Travel and rest days from the game schedule (per-game weather is now covered in Inning 4).
    • Umpire ball/strike calls with and without crowds (2020 vs 2019).
